Quiet pill summarizing the crew: live crewmate glyphs + "n working · n idle".
```jsx
<CrewStrip working={3} idle={5}/>
```
Place at a screen edge; this is the only crew UI most users ever see.
In the app it is the `Crew` panel of the left side rail, under the activity feed, hugging its content inside the host's panel chrome; with nobody in the crew there is no panel.
