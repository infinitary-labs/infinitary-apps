# French (`fr`)

This file is read by anyone in France who uses Infinitary, most of whom have never opened a terminal.
The register is calm, plain and courteous French, the way a careful colleague writes, never cute and never salesy.
The person is addressed as "vous", with "votre/vos" for what belongs to them, and buttons and commands use the infinitive ("Ouvrir", "Afficher la carte suivante") while instructions in sentences use the imperative ("Choisissez", "Appuyez").
Where grammar forces a gender, the masculine default is used ("capitaine", "le second", "l’auteur", "équipier"), and a feed line about a task agrees with "tâche" in the feminine ("Fusionnée", "Démarrée").
Quotation marks are « » with a no-break space inside, a no-break space sits before ":" and a narrow no-break space before "?" and ";", the apostrophe is the typographic ’, and a dash between clauses is a spaced en dash " – ".
A no-break space sits between a number and its unit or the percent sign ("1,6 Go", "40 %", "3 min"), with a decimal comma and French units (Ko, Go); the plural forms are "one", "many" and "other", and "many" repeats the "other" wording because the count always reaches the message as digits ("1 000 000 lignes").
