# Simplified Chinese (`zh-Hans`)

The reader is a person in mainland China who uses a Mac or a desktop computer daily but has never opened a terminal.
The register is calm, plain written Mandarin as in Apple's and GitHub's own Simplified Chinese interfaces, never cute, never salesy, with no exclamation marks.
The person is addressed as 你, never 您, and is called 船长 where the English says captain.
Sentences use full-width punctuation (，。：；？“”——……), and the "·" separator and "→" arrow stay as in English.
A space sits between Chinese and Latin words, digits or filled placeholders (在 GitHub 上, 剩余 {percent}%, 由 {label} 修改), and no space sits beside full-width punctuation.
Counts take a measure word after the number (3 个部分, 2 条消息, 1 张卡片), and units keep their usual symbols (1.6 GB, 45%).
