# Traditional Chinese, Taiwan (`zh-Hant`)

The reader is a person in Taiwan who has never opened a terminal, reading a calm desktop app written in Taiwan's own computing vocabulary (檔案, 設定, 專案, 程式, 預設, 帳號, 登入), never a character conversion of the Simplified file.
The register is plain and warm, addressing the person as 你 (never 您), the way Taiwan's own consumer apps talk, with no exclamation marks, no 喔 or 唷, and no apology.
Sentences use full-width punctuation (，。：；？「」『』…), and quotes are 「」 with 『』 nested inside.
A half-width space separates Chinese from Latin words, product names and numbers (例如「已登入 GitHub」「剩餘 {percent}%」), except where a unit or a risk word fuses with the number in ordinary Chinese, such as「低風險」.
The ship metaphor keeps its Chinese naval words: the person is 船長, the agent they talk to is 大副, and the helpers are 船員.
Words the person meets on GitHub follow what Taiwan's developers and Microsoft's Taiwan docs use, and "pull request" stays in English because GitHub shows it in English in Taiwan.
