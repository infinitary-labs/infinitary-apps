title: Automation

An automation is a prompt your first mate gets on a schedule.
You write what to tell your first mate, and pick when.
When the time comes, your first mate gets the prompt and acts on it as if you had just sent it.

You can make one in Settings, or tell your first mate what to run and when.

## Where automations live

Open **Settings**, then **Automation**.
You can also search for **Open Automation** in the command search.

The list on the left shows every automation with its state:

- **Running** runs at its next time.
- **Draft** is saved but never runs until you turn it on.
- **Paused** keeps its settings but does not run.
- **Done** is a one-time automation that has run and is waiting for your first mate to handle it.

Pick an automation to read or change it.
**Search automations** finds one by its name, its prompt or when it runs.

## Make an automation

1. Open **Settings**, then **Automation**, and choose **New automation**.
2. Give it a **Name**, such as "Weekly review".
3. Under **When**, pick how often it runs.
4. Under **Project**, pick the project it is about, or leave **No project**.
5. Under **Tell your first mate**, write the prompt.
6. Choose **Save draft** to keep it for later, or **Turn on…** to check it and turn it on.

**Preview** shows the next three times it will run, and the prompt as your first mate will get it.
Nothing is sent.

## When it runs

- **Repeats** runs every so many minutes, at most every 15 minutes.
- **Every day** runs at a time of day.
- **On chosen days** runs on the weekdays you pick, at a time of day.
- **Once** runs at one date and time.
- **When something happens** runs each time something new happens, such as **A task closes**, or an event from something you connected in Settings.

A daily or weekly automation keeps the **Time zone** you pick, so it runs at the same time wherever your computer is.

The **Insert** buttons under the prompt each put a detail in it, such as the time it was planned for.
Each detail is filled in when the prompt is sent.

## Turn it on

A new automation from Settings starts as a draft.
A draft never runs.

When you choose **Turn on…**, the page shows what will happen before anything runs:

- when it runs and in which time zone,
- the project and the prompt exactly as your first mate gets it,
- the next times it runs,
- what it sends: the prompt and its schedule details go to your AI subscription and stay in your first mate's conversation, and nothing else gets new access,
- what happens to a missed time.

Choose **Turn on** to start it, or **Back** to return with nothing changed.

Your first mate can make automations too.
A repeating automation your first mate makes on its own waits as a draft until you turn it on.

## Pause, change or delete

- **Pause** stops a running automation.
  A prompt it had not yet delivered is taken back.
- **Resume** starts it again from its next time.
  It does not catch up on the time it was paused.
- **Pause all** pauses every running automation at once.
  It leaves drafts alone.
  You can also search for **Pause all automations** in the command search.
- **Delete**, then **Delete for good**, removes it with its history.
  The tasks and cards it led to stay.

## When Infinitary is closed

Automations run only while Infinitary is open and the computer is awake.
If a time passes while it is closed, the automation runs once when Infinitary opens again, and the prompt says how many times it missed.

## History

On an automation's page, the **History** tab lists its runs, newest first.
Each run shows where it stands:

- **Waiting**: your first mate has not handled it yet.
- **Retrying**: your first mate could not take it yet, and it is being tried again.
- **Handled**: your first mate handled it.
- **Cancelled**: it was taken back before your first mate read it, for example because you paused the automation.

A handled run shows what your first mate found, after **Found:**, and the tasks and cards it **Led to**.
History keeps runs for 30 days.

## Notes

An automation can keep **Notes** that its runs write for later runs, so the next run starts faster.
A crewmate an automation starts edits them, and you can edit them too.
They are advice, never instructions.
**Earlier versions** shows each past version and who wrote it.

## Kept running

Under the list, **Kept running** shows jobs a crewmate set up at your ask that keep running after their task closed.
**Check** looks at whether a job still runs, and **Remove** takes it away.

## The Automation panel

Once you have an automation, the **Automation** panel shows on the side of the window.
It lists each automation's state and next run.
Click one to open it in Settings.
See [Panels](/panels/).
