Infinitary on iPhone and iPad shows the first mate on your computer.
Your first mate, its tasks and their work stay on your computer.
The phone shows its cards, tasks and Activity, and sends your messages and answers there.

It needs iOS 18 or later.

## Getting it

Infinitary for iPhone and iPad is in private TestFlight.
[Email help@getinfinitary.com to request an invitation](mailto:help@getinfinitary.com?subject=Infinitary%20TestFlight%20request).

## Join your computer

1. On your computer, open Settings → **Devices** and press **+ A phone**.
   A code to scan appears, with its link beside it.
2. Open Infinitary on your phone.
   It welcomes you with three ways in: **Scan the code**, **Paste its link** and **Join by its address**.
3. Choose **Scan the code** and point your camera at the code on your computer.
4. Check the computer's name, then press **Join** and that name.

The code works once, for ten minutes.
If the camera is not allowed, paste the link instead.
**Join by its address** takes the address you use with SSH for your computer, such as `you@studio.local`, when Remote Login is on there.

Your phone and your computer need to share a network: the same Wi-Fi, or the same private network when you are away.
Only join your own computer.

## What it shows

The top bar has **Home**, **Tasks** and **Activity**.

- **Home** shows your cards, one at a time; swipe to the next.
  Answer a card on the phone as you would on your computer.
- **Tasks** lists your tasks in the same groups as your computer, such as **Waiting on you**, **Working** and **Queued**.
  Tap a task to open its card or its record, where you can start or continue it.
- **Activity** shows what happened, newest at the bottom.

When your panels are on, a fourth tab, **Panels**, shows them one under another.

## Read a card on an open foldable iPhone

With **Split at the fold** on under Settings → Appearance, Infinitary folds the side panel while you read a card, leaving more room for the card.
Use **Show panels** next to Settings to bring back Tasks, Activity and your panels, or **Fold panels** to return to the card.
Panels you show stay open as new cards arrive.
If the panel would fold automatically while you type, Infinitary waits until the keyboard closes.
When no card is open, the panel shows.
Touch the card to tuck away the last reply, or tap the message box to see it again.
**Maximize card**, in the card's corner, also hides the message box.

On a landscape iPad, the sidebar stays unless the card needs more room.
When you put the card aside, the sidebar returns if the next card fits or no card is open.
It also returns when the window widens enough.

## Talk to your first mate

The message box sits at the foot of **Home**.
Type, or use your keyboard's own dictation, and attach photos from your library.
Your last messages and your first mate's replies show above the box.

## Settings on the phone

The phone's Settings shows your computer's **Projects**, **Intelligence**, **Runbooks**, **Usage** and **Automation**, in your computer's own words.
When your computer has an update ready, the phone can start it, after it asks you first.
See [Update and remove Infinitary](/update-and-remove/#your-other-computers-and-your-phone).

## When your computer is out of reach

The phone needs your computer awake, with Infinitary open.
If it cannot reach it, it says "Your computer is out of reach. Check that Infinitary is open and both devices share a network."

The phone tells you about a new card only while the app is open.

## Leave or remove

To stop showing your computer, tap the computer button at the right of the phone's top bar and choose **Leave** with your computer's name.
The phone forgets the computer.
On your computer, **Remove** on the phone's row in Settings → **Devices** cuts it off at once.
To join again, make a new link.

See also [Your other computers](/your-other-computers/).
