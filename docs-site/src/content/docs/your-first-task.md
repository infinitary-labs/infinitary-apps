title: Your first task
description: A walk through one task, from typing what you want to merging the change.
---

This page follows one ask from start to finish.
It assumes a subscription is connected and your project is added.
If not, start with [First launch](/first-launch/).

## 1. Say what you want

Type your ask in the message box at the bottom of the window, then send it.
Write it the way you would ask a person: what you want, and anything that matters to you.
For example: "On the website, make the sign-up button easier to see on phones."
[What you can ask for](/what-you-can-ask-for/) shows more kinds of asks.

You can also talk instead of typing, or add pictures and files.
See [The first mate](/the-first-mate/).

## 2. Your first mate takes it

Your message appears just above the message box.
Under it, your first mate's mark spins beside "Working…" until its reply takes that place.
The reply is one short sentence saying what it did, such as starting a task.

A question it can answer itself comes back as a card.
Work on your project becomes a task, with a key such as `WEB-12`, and a crewmate starts on it.
Every ask gets a record in Tasks, on the right of the window.

## 3. A crewmate does the work

The crewmate works in its own working copy of your project, so your own files stay as they are.
While it works, it orbits your first mate in the middle of the window.
Short lines about what it is doing appear in Activity, on the left.

You do not need to watch.
To see more, open the task from Tasks, or rest the pointer on the crewmate.
See [Tasks and crewmates](/tasks-and-crewmates/).

If the crewmate needs a decision from you, it brings a card.
For a bigger ask, that card may be a plan for your yes before it builds.
Answer it, and the crewmate carries on.

## 4. The merge card

When the work is done and checked, the crewmate brings a merge card.
It is the change's whole story:

- **Why**, in a sentence.
- **What changed**, as a few short points.
- **Before** and **After** pictures of each screen the change touches.
- What the crewmate checked, and how.
- A small drawing of the change's risk, at the card's top right.

Click a picture to see it larger.
Rest on a dot of the risk drawing to read what it rates and why.
See [Cards and decisions](/cards-and-decisions/) for more on reading a card.

## 5. Merge it

Read the card, then choose:

- **Merge the pull request** merges the change where your project lives.
  For a project kept on this computer, the button reads **Merge the branch**.
- **Review carefully**, on a pull request, asks for an independent review first.
  A new merge card comes back once the review passes.
- **Discard** drops the work.

If the checks are still running, the button reads **Merge when checks pass**.
Click it, and Infinitary merges as soon as they pass.
If the checks failed, the button reads **Merge after fixing errors**.
Click it, and Infinitary merges once the errors are fixed and the checks pass.

To ask for a change instead, type in the card's field, which reads "anything to add?", and press **Chat**.
The crewmate answers in a thread beside the card, and updates the card when it has finished.

## 6. After the merge

The task closes and moves to Done in Tasks.
Its record keeps what you asked, what changed, the evidence and what you decided.

If the change needs something only you can do after it merges, the merge card says so at its top, under "After merging, you need to…".
See [Cards and decisions](/cards-and-decisions/).

## Merging without asking

A project asks before merging until you say otherwise.
To let finished work merge once it passes the checks, turn off **Ask before merging** for that project in Settings → Projects.
You still get a card with the change's story after it merges.
