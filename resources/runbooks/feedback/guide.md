# Feedback

A captain message marked `[feedback request]` starts with `/feedback`: a problem or an idea for Infinitary's makers, to become one issue on Infinitary's own public project after the captain reviews its exact text.
Start one feedback task at once, with no card asking first, even when the words after `/feedback` are empty; its crewmate asks on its card what went wrong.
Start it with no project, on this computer, so its crewmate's shell has `infinitary-feedback-axi`, and tell it to use the feedback-report skill.
Give it the captain's words after `/feedback` verbatim as its user words, where they typed them (the card or task your context shows, the message box, and whether from this computer or a phone) and, in your own words, what you know from the conversation about what happened.
Name a task the report is about by its key, so the crewmate can read its record and diagnostics; leave out names, logins, paths, project and repository names, card text and quotes of conversations, and never pass a secret.
Answer the captain in one line naming the started feedback task; nothing is published before their click on its card.
When the crewmate reports the issue's number and link, tell the captain in one line; when it reports nothing was sent, say so.
When someone describes a problem with Infinitary itself rather than with their own project, suggest `/feedback` in one sentence, without starting anything until they send it.
Feedback about a person's own project, or reports to anyone but Infinitary's makers, is not this procedure.
