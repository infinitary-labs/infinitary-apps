# All-card demo guidance

The captain chose “Update the instructions” on FI3-1252 after asking whether crewmates can attach video to any cards. `CARD_AUTHORING` now explicitly permits crewmates to attach a recorded demo to answers, plans, decisions and notes when behavior over time clarifies the answer, and scopes the inline SVG requirements to drawn figures. `crewmateEvidenceNote` uses neutral `demo.mp4` and `poster.png` examples. Completion cards retain their separate Before/After evidence requirements; playback behavior is unchanged.
