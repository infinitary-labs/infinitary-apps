INF-3170 piece 2, approved by the captain with its carry "That task, its reviewer, a stronger-model retry and a takeover of its work".

The context block's crew line gains `allowed`, each yes's card and what it covers, and the by-key read carries each yes with the captain's words.
The crewmate side, outside the first mate's contract: `emit_card` gains `allows`, `RIGOR_SAFETY` points at the list, the brief and the reviewer's brief carry it, and a `task-allowed-changed` notice serves it on change.
