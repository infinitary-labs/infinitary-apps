# Available authors show their own answers

FI3-1162, approved with Approve and build, makes answer ownership general rather than bug-specific.
Firstmate steers an active author to show what the captain must see or decide on that author's own card, never asking for report only so Firstmate can relay the answer.
When a reply under Firstmate's card needs a crewmate, that crewmate shows its answer on its own card and Firstmate answers the thread with one line naming it.
An investigation's captain choice closes its answer task; Firstmate does not resolve it on the captain's behalf.
Internal-step briefs use the same see-or-decide rule as every crewmate and explain that replies and choices reach the author directly.
Crew-wide, permission, closed-record and reviewer questions remain with Firstmate, as do repair caps and continuation coordination.
The core wording adds about 17 fixed-context tokens; the card and handoff contracts, tool permissions and sequence seed are unchanged.
