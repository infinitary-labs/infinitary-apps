# The approved plan settles a review finding

INF-6608's plan, built by its follow-on task: a reviewer read the approved plan, recommended the plan's own way for a finding, and still asked the captain, because its rule let a builder's stated reason outrank the plan, and its card step checked only the user's words and the decisions so far.
The captain chose a careful review of part 1 and no first mate screen of each question.

- The reviewer's brief (`renderReviewBrief`): a finding the approved plan or the user's words answer for that very case is auto-fix even against a choice the builder gave a reason for, since a builder's easier, cleaner or shorter way never reopens the plan; behavior the plan did not ask to change stays as on the default branch, so restoring it is such a fix.
  The old clause "nor, unless a project rule answers it, one against a deliberate choice the builder gave a reason for" is removed, and the simplification sentence makes a deliberate design ask-user only where the plan and the user's words leave it open.
- The ask-user list gains an approved plan that cannot be built as written, would break a project rule or a standing direction, would lose saved data or weaken security, contradicts another part of itself, or rests on a fact that changed since its approval.
- An ask-user finding on plan-built work quotes the plan's nearest sentence and says why it does not settle the case, or which fact means the plan must change.
- The Review finding round (`REVIEW_CARD_RULES`) names the approved plan among what settles a finding, and a finding whose recommended way is the plan or the user's words as written is recorded again as auto-fix at the same head, which sends it to the builder, and the reviewer raises no card that turn, since the next handoff carries only the findings still left for the captain.
- A reviewer's brief names the task its builder is built on (`renderReviewedPlanSection`), with its key, title, saved card file and the captain's choice and answers, and leaves to the reviewer whether it is a plan the captain approved, since a verified basis may be a reproduction; "Decided so far" carries the captain's answers on that task's cards.
- The crewmate's card rules add one sentence after the answered-card sentence: the approved plan the work is built from settles what it names in the same way.

The crewmate's fixed context grows by one sentence of 15 words; the reviewer's brief by about 150 words, plus the plan section when the builder has a plan.
