# An automation whose every look fails for an hour is a notice

A session audit found three automations that kept reading a project's old address on its host and still showed as on: every look failed, nothing reached the first mate, and nobody was told.
The captain chose to build the audit's proposal: when every look has failed for an hour, the automation shows as stopped in Settings with the reason, and the first mate hears once.

What the first mate is now told:

- An `automation-looks-failing` wake names the automation and its id, the integration and event, since when every look failed, how many looks failed in a row, and the newest answer as words from outside.
  It says Settings shows the automation as stopped, that it keeps looking and runs again by itself, and asks the first mate to tell the captain once on a note, or to offer a fix it can make itself on a decision card.

Home: the wake alone; nothing else is said twice.
The host decides only the typed fact - an hour of ticks with at least two failed looks - and reads no word of the answer.
The fixed context does not grow: the new words ride only this wake.
