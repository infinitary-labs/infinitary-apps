# A held batch's Try again on the first mate's judgment

The captain asked why trying a held batch again should wait for their word, after a fix declared for a held batch never joined it and the first mate's retry of that batch answered with an error.
The typed-words rule came in with batches themselves and no reason for it was recorded.

The `app_change` catalog's `project-batch-answer` moves from the own-words tier to the judgment tier, so it leaves the tool description's list of actions that need the captain's typed words.
Its line now tells the first mate it tries a held batch again on its own judgment, giving its reason, with every change waiting in line, and sends changes back only on the captain's own words, as before.
The one home of the rule is that catalog line; the line grows by about 10 tokens of fixed context, and the own-words list shrinks by about 6.
