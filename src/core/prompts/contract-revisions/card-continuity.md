# Declared card continuity

FI3-1466's approved lesson plan selects leave-on-choice, not a held waiting drawing.
`emit_card` offers the generic `holds_place` declaration to either author: replies keep the card shown, and a newly arriving marked card comes forward even while another is shown.
Every choice still closes its card immediately, and paging away is respected on revisions and thread answers.
The host stores the declaration, not a lesson-specific type or inferred reading of the body.
The tool tells both authors to leave the declaration unset unless the use case requires continuous attention on the card.
The width parameter and effect wording are shortened without removing any rule, paying for the declaration and default-unset instruction within the existing fixed-context ceiling.
Teaching, warm lesson voice and the lessons guide are subsequent pieces of the approved plan.
