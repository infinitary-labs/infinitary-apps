INF-1879, approved direction C with the minimize icon: putting any card away never ends work, decides a question or refuses consent.
The answer brief is the one home for marking an answer note and the host’s explicit Done receipt.
The shared ordinary-note acknowledgement rule lives in `ORDINARY_NOTE_READ_GUIDANCE` in `cardContract.ts`, served by the read wake, card guidance and report receipts; report fields reuse its acknowledgement clause.
The host marks an actual ordinary read on its wake; the author hears that acknowledgement and reports finished if nothing remains, without the read itself closing work.
Legacy and duplicate-close wakes do not acquire that acknowledgement.
Done on a saved answer note, or its record after it was put away, closes the task answered; no elapsed time does so.
Report guidance and receipts distinguish ordinary-note acknowledgement from putting decisions or pending notes aside and reading marked saved answers; those are not acknowledgement, and saved answers still await Done.
The fixed card guidance carries the distinction once; compact report fields reuse only its acknowledgement clause and keep the existing fixed-context budget.
