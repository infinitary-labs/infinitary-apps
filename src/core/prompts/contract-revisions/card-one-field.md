# One field under a card, with Chat

INF-924, built on the captain's Approve and build of the prototype he chose: the Anything else? box and the reply row become one field above a card's buttons, with a Chat button at the right edge of the button row.
The captain's words: "after typing, pressing "chat" or "enter" will send the reply without making a choice. pressing any other choice will send the text along with the choice."
Words sent by Chat or Enter reach the author as a reply, as before; words left in the field at a click on any other button ride that click as the answer named `anything_else`, marked written in, on the path INF-680 gave them.
A reply no longer carries those words as a setting beside it: the words are the reply.

A crewmate's card authoring text (`CARD_AUTHORING`) rewrites its one sentence on the box to say this, and the card lint's refusal of an author's control named `anything_else` names the field instead of the box.
The Firstmate's fixed context stays as it was: the sentence is crew-only, and the Firstmate reads the answer by the rule it already has for an answer marked written in.
