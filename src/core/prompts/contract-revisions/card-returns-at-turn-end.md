# A card comes back when its author's turn ends

INF-926, grounded on INF-797, where the captain asked that a card come back "when the crewmate's turn is finished", not while the crewmate is still updating it.
A card the captain has already seen - replied under, put aside, or revised while shown - now comes back or redraws at its crewmate's turn end, with its last write; a report mid-turn no longer brings it back, and new cards still go up at the report.
CARD_REPLY now says the captain sees answers when the author's turn ends, in place of "at report or turn end", and the crewmate's thread steer preamble says the same in place of "only when you report".
The first mate's own cards keep their rule: its turn's end is its close-out.
The revised words cost about the same tokens as the words they replace.
