# The care is a name; what it asks is the Care levels runbook's

INF-1876, piece 5 of INF-1868's plan, on the captain's words: "the host only knows the three names as typed fact - a metadata. how to execute each care level becomes something the runbooks cover".
The Firstmate's core now says that a review is an operation on a change at a version, at any care - when a builder's report or the captain asks for one, `spawn_crewmate` with `review_of` naming that task and the user's words, the host briefing the reviewer on the builder's branch and its wakes naming each later step - and that what each care asks is the care guidance's, never a hold of the host's.
It no longer says the host holds a Careful push or merge until a review passes, because no such hold exists: the `review` and `live-proof` holds leave the registry, and a completion card is refused only on the checks.
The findings wake carries the fixer preamble (`REVIEW_FIX_RULES`) on every review, so no brief carries it; the builder's brief carries one care line, the name and a pointer to the care guidance served with its instructions (`renderRigorSection`).
The Care levels runbook (`resources/runbooks/care`) serves the Firstmate its guide - what to do when a Careful builder reports committed and asks for its review, how to relay findings, when to steer the push and when to tell the builder to emit its card - and serves the crew their section by the care's name: Quick its one sentence, Default the procedure, Careful the procedure and the careful-change skill.
A policy notice that changes the care carries the sections the new facts select that the crewmate's instructions did not, under one heading (`POLICY_GUIDANCE_HEADING`, `noticeWakes.ts`), so a rise to Careful mid-task serves the Careful section where the host once held the merge.
The core gives up the Careful hold sentences and gains the one review sentence; `tests/firstmate-fixed-context.test.ts` measures the fixed context and the ledger there names where each rule the core gave up is still served.
The Firstmate's fixed context measures about 19,100 tokens after this revision, under the 19,772 boundary and smaller than before it, since the core gave up more words than the one sentence costs; a crewmate's instructions at Careful on Claude measure about 26,600 tokens with its tools, under the 26,624 crewmate boundary (`tests/crewmate-fixed-context.test.ts`).
