# Every computer is a computer, and a phone is a phone

FI3-1440, piece 2 of the Linux plan (FI3-1432), which the captain agreed with "computer" for every computer, Macs included, and "phone" for a phone (question 6, "computer everywhere").
The words the Firstmate is served that named this computer or another device "Mac" now read them from one owner, `src/shared/deviceNames.ts`: "this computer" for the computer it runs on, "another computer", "a computer" and "computers" for the rest, and "the computer where Firstmate lives" where a device names its home.
The changed lines are the `app_change` action lines `project-runs-on`, `device-invite`, `device-remove`, `device-update`, `device-sign-in`, `device-address` and `personal-resources` ("each computer's own global instructions and skills"), the persona's rule on a project kept on this computer with no sign-in, and the cards and refusals those actions write.
Nothing else changes: the saved ids keep their names, so `this-mac` still names this computer as a task's Runs on, the `mac` device kind keeps its id, and a macOS user, Remote Login and System Settings keep the names macOS gives them.
The new words cost a few tokens, since "computer" is five letters longer than "Mac" on each line it replaces.

The ledger entry that comes with it:

FI3-1440. The words the Firstmate is served say "this computer", "another computer" and "the computer where Firstmate lives" where they said "this Mac", "another Mac" and "the home Mac", read from src/shared/deviceNames.ts; saved ids, the mac kind and this-mac keep their names.
