# Window contact is not work contact

INF-4835, piece 2: `app_state` for `device-sign-in` gives work reach, window contact when applicable, the typed setup reason and its explanation when known, and whether the computer reported its sign-ins.
A sign-in card is not offered when work is out of reach or has not reported its sign-ins.
If the read did not answer, the facts include `signInsUnanswered` and a refusal says the computer may need the home version.
These are live tool results; the fixed context is unchanged.
