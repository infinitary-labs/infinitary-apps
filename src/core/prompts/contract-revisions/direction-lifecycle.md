# Review conditional directions without dispatching trigger checks

The session audit INF-1256 found two covered directions retired only after the captain asked, and a conditional direction dispatched as INF-1210 before its installed-build condition held.
The first mate reviews the complete directions it already receives against current turn facts, including reports and updates.
Evidence of an ended condition or a built-in rule fully covering a direction earns a retirement offer naming the direction and evidence, with Retire and Keep.
An unresolved offer stays on its card; a kept direction is not offered again without new evidence.
An unmet or unknown condition stays in the direction text, without a task to wait for or check it, and is reconsidered when relevant facts arrive.
A fulfilled condition makes a direction applicable to authorized work, not authorization for new work.
Judgment remains with the first mate: no parser, trigger store, timer, action tier or retirement permission changes.
The five disposable live evaluation cases cover covered, ended, unmet, fulfilled and still-valid directions through the production seat, task and direction tools.
