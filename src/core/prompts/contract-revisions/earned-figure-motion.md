# A press shows what a still drawing cannot hold

FI3-869's saved direction A, now requested for implementation, with previews exempt and the brief's figure-for-each-part sentence unchanged.
`CARD_AUTHORING` tests what a press adds rather than naming subjects that must play.
A figure plays only when a press shows what one still drawing cannot hold: the same thing in two places, as a card moving through a stack or crew arriving on a ring, or more states than panels would fit.
A figure with one value the reader would vary keeps one control that redraws it.
Everything else stays still, including timelines, charts, numbered steps and flows; a cursor or highlight sweeping a drawing that already shows every state adds nothing.
Previews stay exempt because they draw something that does not exist yet.
Motion still starts on a press or drag, makes one pass, never starts on arrival or loops, keeps its script inside its figure and its explanation in resting markup, and cuts when the duration tokens read zero.
The design skill and card-authoring page carry the same rule; the recipe demonstrates a width control instead of stepping through already visible passing runs.
No host behavior changes, and no existing card is rewritten.
The new words cost the fixed part about 66 tokens by the app's count; with them it measures 18,123, and the current 18,500 ceiling has room, so it does not move.
