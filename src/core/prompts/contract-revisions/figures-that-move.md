# A figure moves only for what moves or varies

FI3-796's answer, the captain's Approve and build, with his yes to "a script inside a figure keeps the card an ordinary body".
He asked whether some real cards would be better explained by an animated or interactive figure, and how to tweak the instructions for the right balance; of twelve real figure cards, five explained a change over time or a value the reader would vary.
`CARD_AUTHORING`'s script sentence now says a script may make a figure move, and when: a figure of a change over time - events in order, a state that moves, a queue, a motion - plays; a figure with one value the reader would vary - a width, a count, a moment - takes one control that redraws it; every other figure stays still.
Motion runs on the captain's press or drag, one pass from one state to the next, never on arrival and never in a loop; a moving figure's script sits inside its `<figure>`; the resting markup is the whole explanation, its labels in the markup where the size check reads them, and when `--dur-med` and `--dur-slow` read zero every move is a cut.
The build added two clauses to the approved wording: where the script sits, because only a script inside a figure keeps the card prose, and the two duration tokens' names, because no author can read the frame's stylesheet.
In the host, `isPreviewBody` no longer counts a script inside a figure, so such a card keeps the shape lint, the change bars, the Firstmate's excerpt and the width rule, and the scripted vehicle now draws change bars.
The new words cost the fixed part about 156 tokens by the app's count; with them it measures 17,958, under the 18,000 budget.
