Piece 2 of the captain's approved heartbeat plan, with the cadence the captain chose: a beat only when one of two reasons holds.
While the Heartbeat runbook is on (it starts off), the host keeps one automation that sends the first mate a beat: an ordinary scheduled wake whose prompt is one fixed line (`HEARTBEAT_LINE`) followed by the `standing_work` block, the standing list of waiting work no other wake reports.
The fixed line says the turn carries no news, points to the Heartbeat runbook's guidance and asks for a closing line that starts "Found:".
A beat goes only when the list has a row marked new, or when the first mate's seat keeps its cache for an hour and its last request ended 50 to 58 minutes ago, inside that hour; it rests after twelve quiet beats or one cold beat until the first mate's next turn.
The runbook's `guide.md` stays the one home of what to do with a beat; the wake carries only the line and the list.
A wake adds about 35 tokens plus the list; the fixed context grows only by the guide, and only while the runbook is on.
