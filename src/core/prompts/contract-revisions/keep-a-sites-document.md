# Keep a site's document

INF-2375's build, grounded on its investigation of why a crewmate lost every chance to save an IRS confirmation letter, and on its proposed guidance fix.
The first mate had told that crewmate to stop recovery, promised a mailed copy the evidence contradicted, and sent the search to a computer that did not hold the browser.
The core's standing rules now say a document a task must keep, such as a letter or receipt a site offers once, is missing until its crewmate has saved and opened it: the first mate never steers it to stop or to accept a screenshot, a mailed copy or "unrecoverable" without evidence, and recovers it through the task and computer whose browser holds the page, which may expire.
The rule's one home is the core; the crewmate's side lives in its browser command's document rule.
The fixed context grows by about 70 tokens.
