# Knowledge replaces notes

FI3-1601, under the captain's revised scope: carry only active directions, with no older-note archive or full historical migration.
Every build serves Knowledge, including stable.
`remember` and `forget` tools retire; their natural requests use `record_knowledge` for a direction or personal context.
Directions are a distinct kind with captain-only publish, revise and retire, and `knowledge get` reads them by `d:` identifier.
The legacy source and original-note tool reads retire.
Every Firstmate turn carries the complete current direction set independently of search, with instruction/advice labels and no implied permission.
Crew receive their project's current directions on fresh, resumed and placed turns, never private global directions.
New work pauses if those directions cannot be read.
The fixed tool surface shrinks by removing two tools; no fixed-context allowance increase is requested.
