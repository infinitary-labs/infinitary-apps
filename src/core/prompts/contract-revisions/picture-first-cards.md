# Cards open with pictures and fold their long text

INF-3749, the captain's ask for "fewer words, big visuals": long text is supporting material when truly needed, not the default, and most of it hides in an expand.
The captain chose the Picture first version of the plan.

What the first mate and every crewmate are now told, in the card rules (`CARD_AUTHORING`):

- Each part of an explanation gets its own figure, and a figure carries its own names and numbers, the deciding one at display size.
- The open body is the answer, the figures, one sentence beside each, and any questions; reasons, steps, caveats, a builder's spec, evidence and paths fold in a `<details>` whose `<summary>` names it.

The two sentences replace the ones that kept names, numbers, quotes and caveats in the open and let `<details>` hold only evidence, and are 10 characters shorter, so neither fixed context grows.

Homes beside the card rules, each said once: the answer brief (`answerShape`) says the same for an answer card and a step's card, a crewmate's merge outline folds what is left, and the change story's own parameter asks for one Why sentence, one-clause bullets of what a person gains, and any measured number in Before and After.
The host adds no refusal and counts no words a card may hold; the replay report counts open words per card for the team to read.
