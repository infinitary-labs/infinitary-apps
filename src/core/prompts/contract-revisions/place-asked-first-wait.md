# A call its place asked about first waits for that question's outcome

Reaching Gmail and Calendar through Google's MCP servers, where Google can stop a call the captain allowed to ask the captain first.
When the captain's click on the app's approval card runs a call and the place asks first, the app keeps the same call as a new ask on a card with the place's own question, and the ask the click answered ran nothing.

What the first mate is now told:

- The `write-answered` wake for the answered ask says its click sent nothing yet, names the new ask, and says not to call it again, instead of telling it to call the integration again to ask again.
- The new ask's outcome comes once, in a `write-answered` wake of its own.

Home: the `write-answered` wake, from a typed fact the host holds (the new ask's id), never from reading the place's words.
The fixed context does not grow: the new words ride only that wake.
