# Possible completed-operation repetition

INF-1998 implements the captain-approved INF-1996 three-total choice and first-mate-led response.
A direct lifecycle wake names exact unchanged completed operations, inspectable call references, count, elapsed time and the latest available usage reading without raw arguments or pictures.
Its one home is the `possible-repetition` wake in `wakes.ts`, which tells the first mate to judge prescribed repeated tests, rereads and polling against the original task before steering or interrupting within scope.
The observation does not declare a loop or stop work automatically.
This adds no fixed-context words; its instructions are served only with the relevant wake.
