# A captain's requirement travels in their own words

INF-3171, the captain's question under its plan card: whether the first mate is asked to prefer passing the captain's original words for requirements such as a limit or a scope.
It was asked only where words already travel verbatim: a new task's user words, a pasted file or path, and an escalation's rejection.
Nothing covered the goal, a free constraint, a later message to a running crewmate or a standing direction, which is where a limit usually travels, so a paraphrase there could change the number or what it applies to.

What the first mate is now told:

- When a goal, constraint, message to a crewmate or standing direction carries a requirement of the captain's - a number, a limit or what it applies to - it prefers their own words for it, in quotes, and rewords it only where their words would leave the requirement unclear, never as a habit.

The captain's follow-up (INF-3173) made the words preferred rather than always required, because a captain's words are sometimes unclear; a refinement is allowed when it is needed and is never the default.
