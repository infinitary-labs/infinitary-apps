# Review caps go to their declared owner

INF-166 direction A, approved by the captain, delegates a qualifying review cap to the first mate.
The captain selected info asks for the first mate and reopening every time the findings qualify, replacing the proposed once-only limit.
The rule lives in the review-cap wake: its typed owner names whether the first mate may decide and steer without a cap card, or the captain must answer as before.
First-mate wakes carry no captain choices; older queued wakes without an owner retain the captain instruction.
The per-round flow, reviewer brief, round limit, CI cap and merge permission do not change.
No fixed-context words are added.
