# A reworked merge card comes back once its new version's checks end

INF-6241, from the captain's confusion on INF-6224.
The captain replied under a merge card asking for a change.
The crewmate answered in the thread, pushed a new version and ended its turn, so the card came back at once: it still showed the version they replied under, and its strip and Merge read as ready while the new version's checks were still running.
The captain chose that a reworked merge card comes back only once the update is made, its new checks have passed and the card shows it.

What a crewmate is now told, in the thread-reply wake:

- On its merge card, when the captain's words change the work, it posts one interim line, then answers with the revised card once the checks on the version it pushed pass.
- The app keeps the card from the captain until then, and a question the rework raises goes on a card of its own.

Home: the thread-reply wake's preamble, which every crewmate reads when the captain replies under its card; no tool description or brief repeats it.
The hold itself is the host's, read from typed facts (a running background job or turn, the change's read after that work, the change's newer version, a standing check or run watch, a wake the lane can deliver); no word of the crewmate's holds or releases the card (`docs/card-threads.md`, "A reworked merge card").
The first mate's fixed context does not grow: the sentence rides only a crewmate's wake.
