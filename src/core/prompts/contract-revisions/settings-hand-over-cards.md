# Settings hand-over cards

INF-5355, the second piece of INF-5326's approved Settings parity plan, gives the first mate a card for each act in Settings that stays the captain's own click, and two lines that point the captain's window at a place.
The action catalog owns every new word, one line each: `device-invite` takes `how` "phone"; `device-change-address`, `home-move`, `standby-keep`, `device-forge-sign-in`, `keep-home`, `kept-job-forget` and `secrets-page` are hands lines whose cards open the exact place in Settings; `show-task` and `show-settings` are asked lines that open a task's record or a Settings section and item and change nothing.
`integration-add` with kind inbox now offers a card that opens the Infinitary command's switch in Integrations, where it opened a server key form before (INF-5348).
The core, the tool descriptions and the wakes are unchanged; the lines reach the first mate through the catalog the way every other line does.
