# The Anything else? box

A card answer's `written` words may now run to 4,000 characters, as a reply does, where they stopped at 500: the app's own Anything else? box rides a footer click, and a reply's settings, as the answer named `anything_else`, written in (INF-680).
A question's Something else field still holds itself to 500 characters.
An older home refuses a footer click or a reply whose box words run past 500 characters, and the card stays with the words in its box for the captain to shorten.
