Adds an optional `skipped: true` to a card answer (`CardAnswer`): a question the captain may skip and left untouched, whose `values` and `labels` hold the Default the skip sent, as the card proposed it.
It rides `inf:cards:act`'s value and `inf:cards:reply`'s answers, a card's saved answers, a reply's answers in its thread and a past card's answers.
A home without the change reads answers strictly, so a desktop view or a phone leaves `skipped` out of what it sends there when the home's welcome does not list the change, and the home reads the Default as an answer kept as proposed.
A desktop view or a phone whose hello does not list the change reads answers strictly too, so a home leaves `skipped` out of the thread lines and past cards it sends there.
