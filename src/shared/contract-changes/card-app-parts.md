A card body may name one of the app's own parts, such as the architecture map, with an empty `<script data-inf-part="map"></script>`, and every reader puts its own build's code for the part in place of it.
A computer that runs work sends such a card to a home that does not list this change with the part's code put into the body, and refuses it when the body would then be over the card size limit.
A view or a phone without the change shows the card with the part's markup and none of its code.
