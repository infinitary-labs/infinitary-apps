Adds two optional lists to a card (`Card`): `reads`, the read-catalog names its body's own script may subscribe to while the card is open, and `sql`, the published views its `sql` read runs under.
Only a crewmate's card carries them, checked against the panels' reads and the published views when it is emitted, and a revision without them takes them away.
They ride `inf:cards:list` and the `inf:cards` push, and a placed crewmate's card on `inf:work:card`.
A desktop view whose hello does not list the change reads cards strictly, so a home leaves both lists out of the cards it sends there, and such a view draws the card without live data.
A phone ignores the lists until it answers its card's reads.
A home without the change drops both lists from a placed card, which then draws without live data.
