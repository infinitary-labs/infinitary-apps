# Card origin line

A card's `asked` gains four optional fields: `kind` (`asked`, `said`, `chose`, `automation` or `from`), `choice`, `key` and `through`.
They say where the card's work came from: the captain's message that filed or started it, a message of theirs aimed at that work alone, their click with its button's words, an automation by its name, or the nearest task by key and title, with how many tasks the source sits behind.
An older side reads `at` and `text` alone and draws every line as "You asked" with `text` in the bubble, so an automation's name or a task's title reads as the captain's words there.
