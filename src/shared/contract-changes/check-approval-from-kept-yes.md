`inf:work:report`'s check request in `declared.validation` takes an optional `allowance`: the card of a yes on the task's Already allowed list, or `standing` for the captain's standing override, that covers the check's declared resources and cost.
A home with the change keeps the check's approval from that yes with no new card, while the yes stands.
A home without it drops the field and records an ordinary check request, so the crewmate asks the captain on a card as before.
