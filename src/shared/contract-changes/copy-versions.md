# Each copy says its version

The app open on another computer now says the version of Infinitary it runs in its connection's hello (`appVersion`), as a computer's work already did, and the phone says its own version and build instead of a fixed one.
Each device in `inf:devices:list` and the `inf:devices` push adds `appVersion`, the version of the app open on it now, with `than` (`older` or `newer`) or `same` against the home's own, and `workVersion`, the version its work runs while that is the home's.
A home from before reads a view's `appVersion` as it read it before, for its first mate alone, and its Devices lists no version for an app.
A view from before has no fields of those names and shows no version line.
