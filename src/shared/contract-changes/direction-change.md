# Direction change

`inf:knowledge:direction:set` is a new home-only change: it adds, revises or retires one standing direction, as the app action catalog's `direction` line.
`inf:knowledge:directions` takes its arguments as optional, and with none it lists the first page across every scope, which is the line's current value.
An older home has neither: a side that asks it for the change is refused, and nothing on another computer sends either.
