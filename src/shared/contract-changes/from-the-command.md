A captain's line in a card's thread, a round of the last turn and the `user_turn` push may carry `through: "command"`, when the captain's own command, `infinitary-axi`, sent the words rather than the window.
The window shows "From infinitary-axi" beside such a line.
A view that does not list this change, an older desktop or the phone, receives thread lines without the field, since its thread decoder is strict.
The last turn and the `user_turn` push decode loosely, so an older side ignores the field there and shows the line as the window's.
