Adds an optional `sinceSeen` field to the card of `inf:work:card`: a crewmate's summary of what changed since the captain last saw a merge card for its change, which the home draws first on that merge card.
A computer that runs work sends it only to a home with the change, and refuses the card with words to its crewmate otherwise, so a summary is never dropped without a word.
A home without the change never receives it.
