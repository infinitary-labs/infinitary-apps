# Words from outside there

Adds the order `inf:session:delete-outside`, which a home asks a computer that runs work to replace the words from outside a task's conversation there holds, once due, with their kind's note: the conversation's record, the Claude client's own files for it, and a conversation open there, which it lets go of and opens again from its files.
Its answer names each file it changed under the record's folder, which the home then copies whole.
Adds the optional field `elsewhere` to `inf:retention:delete-outside`'s answer: the conversations on a computer out of reach or not updated yet, which follow once it is back or updated.
A home never asks the order of a computer without this change: it counts that computer's conversations in `elsewhere`, to follow once it is updated.
