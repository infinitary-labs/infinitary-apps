A new read, `inf:settings:own-themes:get`, answers `{ themes }`: each of the person's own color themes worked out, as its id, its name, its base (`dark` or `light`) and every color token the app worked out from its six colors, `--on-accent` included.
A view that holds none of the math, such as the phone, draws a saved theme from these tokens alone and reads them again when the look changes.
An older home has no such read, so a view asks it only of a home that lists this change, and keeps what it last read from one that did.
