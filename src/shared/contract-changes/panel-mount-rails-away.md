# A panel's row says when its rail is out of sight

A row of the window's answer about its panels (`panelMountSchema`, `inf:plugins:personal:mounts`) may carry `away`: `folded` while the rails are folded away for a card or the main message, or `settings` while Settings is open and the window draws no rails.
It rides only a row whose state puts something on a rail.
A window from before sends no `away`, and the core then says nothing of where the rails are, as it did.
