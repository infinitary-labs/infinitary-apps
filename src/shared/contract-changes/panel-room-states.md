# Panels folded for room in the window's answer

`inf:plugins:personal:mounts` admits two more states, `folded` and `gathered`: a panel the rail folded to its header because it is too short for its body, and one it gathered into its More row because it is too short even for its header (`docs/personal-panels.md`, "Placed on the rail").
Only this Mac's own window sends the answer, since its reach is the home, so no paired device sends or reads the new states.
An older window never sends them, and the core then reads a folded panel as drawn and a gathered one as drawn or collapsed, as it did before.
