# Card pushes to a phone

A paired phone may ask the new request `inf:devices:push` to hand its computer its push address and Apple environment, the relay's grant for them and the key it seals with, or null to stop (docs/phone-push.md), and hears whether its computer kept it.
The computer keeps it beside the phone's device entry, forgets it when the phone is removed or leaves, and refuses it from anything but a paired phone.
While it is kept, the computer posts each card the Mac's own notifications would show, sealed under that key, to Infinitary's relay, which wakes the phone.
A computer without this change does not list it, so a phone asks nothing and says that computer needs an update before it can push.
