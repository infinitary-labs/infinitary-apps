# Secret approval task

A waiting use that a phone reads for Secrets approvals may carry `task`, the title of the task that asked, or null when no task asked.
A phone names it in the sentence its own confirmation shows; the card's title, written by the home, already names it.
An older home sends no `task`, and the phone then names the recipient, as before.
An older phone ignores the field.
Each of its secrets may also carry `name`, the name the computer's own card gives it among every saved secret: its place only when another saved secret shares its name, and its reference only when name and place both repeat.
A phone names each secret by it, in its card's foot and in its own confirmation, so both read as the computer's title does; from an older home it names the secret from its label and place, as before.
