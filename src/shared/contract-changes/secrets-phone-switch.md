`inf:secrets:approvals` answers an optional `asked`: the captain turned the asking phone's Approves secret uses switch on in Settings → Devices, so it offers its approval key now.
The Secrets view adds optional `phones`, the paired phones on their way to approving, and each enrolled device's optional `paired` and `stopping`; `inf:secrets:action` takes `ask-phone` and `cancel-phone`.
An older phone ignores `asked`, so the computer's switch waits until the phone's own switch is turned on; an older home omits it, and the phone offers its key only from its own switch.
