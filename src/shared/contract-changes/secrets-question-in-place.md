Adds an optional `question` field to the Secrets view: the one question Settings asks on the row it is about, with its words and the one-time passes that answer it.
Adds an `answer` kind to `inf:secrets:action`, naming one of those passes.
An older window without the change draws no question and sends no answer; a Forget, Remove, Stop approvals or Delete history it sends to a newer home leaves a question it cannot answer, which the next Secrets action drops.
A new member of a closed set would be major by the version rule, but only the computer's own window asks `inf:secrets:action` (reach home), and the window and its home are always one build, so the change stays minor.
