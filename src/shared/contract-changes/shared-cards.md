# Shared cards

Any first mate or crewmate may write on a card that is not its own, with no grant, and the record keeps who did (`docs/card-threads.md`, "Shared cards").
A card order from a computer that runs work may carry the revision its writer read, why it writes, a stable operation identity and the captain message its reply answers; the home answers a write on someone else's card with its typed result.
A reply order may carry the captain message it answers, an operation identity and a correlated response.
The new `inf:work:revise-required-action` order corrects a required action's words, and the new `inf:work:read-card` order reads one card as it stands.
A thread line may carry who contributed it, the captain message it answers and whether it is a clarification on a closed card.
A computer that runs work refuses these writes and reads to a home without this change rather than letting the home drop their fields, and a home answers a typed result to a computer without it as a refusal in words.
A desktop view without this change gets thread lines without the added fields; a phone ignores them.
