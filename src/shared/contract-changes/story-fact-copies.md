A crewmate's saved-story merge card on `inf:work:card` may carry a copy of its story's `riskDeclaration` and `requiredActions`, which the home holds against the saved story: an exact repeat is left out and one that differs is refused.
An older home ignores both fields on a saved-story card, so a computer sends such a copy only to a home that lists this change, and otherwise refuses the card as before, sending nothing.
An older computer refuses those copies itself and never sends them.
