A task's view optionally carries `pinnedAt`, when the captain pinned it to the top of Tasks, and a new request, `inf:task:pin`, pins a task or unpins it on the captain's click or key.
The board lists every pinned task whatever its state or age.
An older side ignores the field and lists a pinned task under its state alone, and an older home refuses the request, so a view's pin writes nothing there.
