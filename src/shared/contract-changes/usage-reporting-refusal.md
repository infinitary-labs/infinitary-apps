# Usage names a company collector that keeps refusing

`inf:usage:reporting` may answer with the optional `refusal`: the host of the company's collector that has taken none of this computer's Claude work for over a minute, and why (`unreachable`, `secure-connection` or `refused`).
Settings → Usage then warns that the work is not reaching that host, in place of the line saying it is reported there.
A view from before ignores the field and keeps the line; a home without this change sends none.
A reason a view does not know reads as `refused` (`VIEW_OPEN_LISTS`).
