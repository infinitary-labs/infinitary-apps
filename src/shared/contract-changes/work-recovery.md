# Work recovery reports

A work report adds optional workWait and recoveryWays fields for owned waits and read-only recovery findings.
Each recovery way may declare its approach: workaround, pass-hold or stronger-model.
Older senders may omit them, and older homes ignore them; neither field authorizes an effect.
A way with no approach reads as passing its hold, so it needs the captain.
A way's hold and approach are words on the wire, not closed sets, so a home and a work computer whose hold registries differ still carry the report.
The home labels a hold or approach its build does not know, such as one INF-1688 retired, as needing the captain, with no basis.
The crewmate's own report tool still offers only its build's holds and approaches.
