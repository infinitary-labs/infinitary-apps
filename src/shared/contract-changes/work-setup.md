# A Mac's window reports its work half

A Mac that joined with its window checks its own background work at every connection to its home, the first of each launch included and never before a welcome, sets up again what is missing, and tells its home what it found with the new request `inf:devices:work-setup`: running, starting, or blocked with a reason (`workSetupReportSchema`, `src/shared/workSetup.ts`).
Each device in `inf:devices:list` and the `inf:devices` push adds `workSetup`, while its window is connected and its work is not in reach: why its work is not running or has not reached the home.
The new request `inf:devices:fix-work` is the Devices row's Fix it: the home sends that Mac's window the live frame `inf:devices:set-up-work`, and the window checks and sets up its work again.
A window sends `inf:devices:work-setup` only to a home that lists this change; a home from before never hears it, and its row reads out of reach as before.
A view from before has no field of that name and shows no reason or Fix it, and a window from before ignores the live frame.
